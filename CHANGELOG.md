# 1.1.0

- Compass: move it anywhere; unlock and reset it from the settings. Right-click while unlocked centres it.
- Compass: size and opacity settings.
- Compass: at the bottom of the screen, distances and tooltips go above it.
- Quests coloured by level, as in the quest log (setting).
- Quests ready for turn-in get a green tick and lead their group.
- Nearby: quest givers with a quest you can take (needs QuestieDB) and flight masters, each with its own range. New ones pulse as they come into range. Click one to go there.
- Your map pin (Ctrl+click on the map) is on the compass and no longer gets replaced.
- HandyNotes: your own notes show on the compass (setting, with a range).
